# Is that water, or just rice?

Most flood apps mistake wet paddy fields for floods. JalDrishti compares today’s radar picture with the dry-season one, then checks the ground height. Result: fewer false alarms, and a clear list of who needs help.

### If water is rising

1\. Move to high ground now.\
2\. Take your kit (see Essentials).\
3\. Avoid walking or driving through moving water.\
4\. Share your live location in SOS.

### Test with your own satellite images

Upload a before and an after picture of the same area, for example Sentinel-1 greyscale exports from Copernicus Browser. JalDrishti marks new dark water and skips water that was already there. It uses a simple log-ratio and Otsu threshold with no terrain mask, so treat results as zones to verify.

### Choosing a safe route

Prefer roads on higher ground and avoid bridges and river crossings. Never cross flowing water. Travel in daylight when you can, in a group, and tell someone your route.

### Safety updates

### Officer playbook

1. Verify flagged roads on the ground first. Flagged means check, not confirmed closed.
2. Send boats or trucks to dry-but-cut-off settlements, not just flooded ones.
3. Keep a duty log of SOS, missing reports and shelters filled.
4. Use ranges when sources disagree and say which source you used.
5. Ask palika volunteers to post geotagged photos to tune the flood map.
